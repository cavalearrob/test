# DTF Design System — V1

Brand: DIRTY THOUGHTS FASHION
Campaign: WHAT DID YOU THINK DTF MEANT?

## Core palette
- Black: #050505
- Hot pink: #FF1682
- White: #FFFFFF

## Product-art rules
- Preserve the original quote/graphic as the product identity.
- Do not add a DTF/Dirty Thoughts Fashion logo to a garment unless that product is specifically a logo product.
- Keep source artwork separate from mockups.
- Never infer or replace missing artwork from a lifestyle/model image.
- Long phrases must be laid out for the actual printable area; do not stretch/crop text across seams.
- Panty/AOP artwork requires its own approved composition rather than reusing shirt artwork.

## Versioning
Approved source designs are immutable. Revisions use v2, v3, etc.
