RETURNS & REFUNDS

Our products are made to order. Please review your size, design, and shipping information carefully before placing an order.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or materially different from what you ordered, contact us promptly with your order information and clear photos so we can review the issue with our fulfillment partner.

Because items are produced specifically for each order, we generally cannot accept returns or exchanges for buyer’s remorse, incorrect size selection, or a change of mind unless required by applicable law.
